Definitions (guide)
  • address: last still frame before the club starts back.
  • top: frame where the club head changes direction at the end of the backswing.
  • impact: frame closest to club-ball contact.
  • finish: first frame of a held, balanced finish; if never held, the last frame of the follow-through.
  • visible: clearly seen, click the joint centre. occluded: partly hidden, click your best estimate from what is visible. not visible: can't locate it; no point. out of frame: outside the picture.
