GolfMe Caddie testing: consent to film and use my golf swing

Form version 1.1 · Please read it all before filming starts. Ask Micky anything that is unclear. You get a copy.

Contributor ID (written by Micky): Contact for questions and withdrawal (written by Micky):

1. What this is

Micky, the developer of the GolfMe app, is testing how accurate GolfMe's swing-analysis feature ("Caddie") is. To do that, he needs videos of real golf swings that qualified golf coaches have checked. This is a small, unpaid test with adult friends. It is not a medical or academic study.

2. What is filmed and recorded

  • Short phone videos (about 10 seconds each) of my full golf swing, from the front ("face-on") and from behind my hands along the target line ("down-the-line"), with a driver, hybrid, or iron. My face and body are visible.
  • Alongside each video: the club, the camera angle, whether I swing right- or left-handed, the lighting, and my own rough skill level (beginner, mid, or advanced).
  • Not recorded: my name, contact details, or where the filming took place. Sound and location data are removed from the videos when they are copied to Micky's computer.
  • My name is kept only on this signed form, stored apart from the videos. The videos are labelled with the Contributor ID above.

3. Why

Only to test and measure the accuracy of Caddie and to tune its settings. My videos will not be used for marketing, posted publicly, shown in the GolfMe app, sold, given to anyone else, or used to train a machine-learning model.

4. Where the videos are kept

On Micky's own encrypted laptop, in a folder that is not synced to any cloud service. They are not uploaded to GolfMe's servers, to cloud storage, or to a chat app.

5. Who sees the videos

Micky. Golf coaches only if I tick a box below. Coaches see my Contributor ID, never my name. Coaches label the videos (for example, the moment of impact, or which swing fault they see) and then judge whether Caddie's feedback is correct.

6. How long they are kept

At most 90 days from the date I sign, until (date written by Micky). Then the videos and everything made from them (coach labels, measurements, still frames) are deleted. Micky may ask me to sign a new form to keep them longer; if I don't, they are deleted on that date.

7. Changing my mind (withdrawal)

  • I can withdraw at any time, for any reason or none, by telling Micky in person, by message, or by email using the contact above. I don't have to explain.
  • Within 7 days, Micky deletes my videos, the coaches' labels of them, and all data made from them, and asks any coach who holds a copy to delete it.
  • Limits I understand: summary numbers already written into a test report (for example "82% of 70 clips") can't be un-calculated, but they don't identify me, and my videos are left out of all later reports. If a video was already sent to an outside service I agreed to in section 9, Micky will ask for deletion there too, but that service's own deletion rules apply.
  • Withdrawing has no effect on any GolfMe account I have, or on my friendship with Micky.

8. Coaches (optional)

Coaches are qualified golf coaches helping with this test. Up to two coaches may watch each video and label it independently.

Coaches may watch my videos in person on Micky's laptop, with Micky present. No copy is given to them. Optional
A coach in another place may also watch my videos (only if the box above is also ticked) by one of these methods: Optional
  • a live video call where Micky shares his screen and plays the video; the call is not recorded and no video file is sent. The video-call service carries the picture live but does not receive a file; or
  • an encrypted USB drive sent by tracked post, with the password sent separately, returned to Micky and wiped afterwards.

9. Outside analysis services (optional, separate)

To compare Caddie's current results with the coaches' labels, Micky may want to run my videos through the outside services Caddie uses today. These are companies other than GolfMe, and they process the video under their own terms. Micky will only send anything after he has checked their data-retention and privacy terms. If I leave a box unticked, my videos are never sent to that service.

Roboflow (Roboflow, Inc., USA) may receive still frames from my videos to detect body positions. Optional
Google Gemini (Google's AI service) may receive my videos, with sound and location removed, to produce swing feedback. Optional
